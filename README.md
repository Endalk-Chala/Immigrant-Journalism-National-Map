# Immigrant Journalism National Map

A state-by-state institutional sampling frame of immigrant and immigrant-serving journalism outlets in the United States.

## Current stage

**Phase 1: National institutional mapping and sampling-frame construction.**

The project identifies and verifies immigrant and immigrant-serving journalism organizations across U.S. states, documents publicly visible institutional characteristics, and builds a transparent sampling frame for later qualitative research on risk ecologies.

## Current pilot states

- Minnesota
- Wisconsin
- Illinois
- Washington
- North Carolina
- Ohio

Planned next expansion: California, Oregon, a deeper Washington pass, Texas, and Alabama.

## Repository structure

- `docs/` — methodology and pilot notes
- `data/candidates/` — candidate outlet registries
- `data/verification/` — manually verified outlet batches
- `data/logs/` — state discovery and search logs
- `scripts/` — conservative public-web collection scripts

## Methodological principle

> **Broad discovery, narrow verification, deeper coding only where analytically useful.**

The database is a **sampling frame, not a risk score**. Missing public information must not be interpreted as evidence that an institution lacks a resource or capacity.

## Public-data boundary

This repository contains only public, non-sensitive research infrastructure. It must not contain private contact information, recruitment records, interview participation status, consent records, interview transcripts, confidential notes, or other human-subjects data.
